Read the measurements responsibly

These are voluntarily shared, unverified community measurements, not model quality scores. CPU/core/memory categories are coarse; equal categories do not imply identical machines. Request and generation timing are not comparable.

Identical submissions are deduplicated; entries are not counts of independent users. Tags may change over time; artifact identity is shown when available. There is no claim of representative or tamper-proof results. Anyone can upload results; pending or rejected submissions remain private.

Confirmed community averages

Anyone can submit results; every report requires admin approval. The table shows arithmetic averages of approved, distinct reports for matching hardware, model artifact, workload, settings and timing. Sample counts are not independent users. Pending and rejected reports never contribute.
