Llameros public benchmarks

Voluntarily shared, unverified community measurements, not model quality scores. CPU/core/memory categories are coarse; equal categories do not imply identical machines. Chat output and embedding input throughput are not comparable. Request and generation timing are not comparable. No accounts, cookies, analytics or client IP database.

Only operator-approved entries are public or used in recommendations. Approval means reviewed, not proven genuine. Six-case token counts and durations are checked and throughput is recomputed. Identical submissions are deduplicated; entries are not counts of independent users. Tags may change over time; artifact identity is shown when available. No claim of representative or tamper-proof results. Uploads require expiring single-use vouchers.
