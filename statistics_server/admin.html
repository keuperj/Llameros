HUMAN REVIEW

Review pushed results

Review hardware, artifact identity, six-case evidence and warning flags before approval. Approval means reviewed, not proven genuine. Approving publishes an entry and permits it to influence recommendations. Rejection removes it from the public API; client caches can take ten minutes to expire. Legacy entries without valid v2 evidence cannot be approved. No contributor identity is collected.
