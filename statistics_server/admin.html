Overview

Allow upload requests

Authorize a contributor by issuing a single-use upload voucher. Deliver it privately, without storing the recipient identity. There is no public identity-bearing request form or automatic approval. Tokens are not linked to entries.

Share only through a private channel. This secret cannot be retrieved later. It clears automatically after five minutes.

Review pushed results

Review hardware, artifact identity, six-case evidence and warning flags before approval. Approving publishes an entry and permits it to influence recommendations. Rejection removes it from the public API; client caches can take ten minutes to expire. Legacy entries without valid v2 evidence cannot be approved.
